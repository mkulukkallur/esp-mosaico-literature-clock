# Changelog

## 1.0.1
- 552 more quotes, from Johannes Enevoldsen's literature clock list (safe-for-work quotes only, CC BY-NC-SA 2.5). They cover 443 minutes that had no quote of their own.
- 1,996 quotes now cover 1,347 of the day's 1,440 minutes.

## 1.0.0
- First release: a quote from a book for the current minute, with the time phrase highlighted and the book and author underneath.
- 1,444 quotes for 904 minutes; other minutes keep the latest earlier quote and show the time in digits.
- Tap for another quote for the same minute; long-press to switch between dark and paper themes.
- Time from NTP over the Wi-Fi saved in Vibe Mode; timezone found automatically or set with `clock_timezone` in Vibe Mode > Settings > Shared information.
- Night dimming (23:00–07:00 by default).
